# PRODUCT
This project is a game called wordis it is meant to capture the feal of tetris attack 
it is a competetive word building game. The game has multiple game modes such as timed, multiplayer, bot, and multiRound



# GAMEPLAY
wordis is a real time spelling game speed is the key to winning 
letters tiles have points and modifiers and each word you spell gives you points according to the points and modifications
players can earn boon cards that can affect their tiles positivly
players can earn attack cards that they can play against their opponent to make the game harder such as locking letters forcing them to use a letter etc.
